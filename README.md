📊 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data using Python to explore sales performance, product categories, profitability, 
customer characteristics, and geographic trends.
The goal is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights through data exploration, cleaning, analysis, and visualization.

🎯 Project Objectives
Explore and understand the dataset.
Clean and prepare data for analysis.
Calculate total sales, total costs, and profit.
Analyze product and sub-category performance.
Understand customer characteristics.
Compare sales performance across countries and states.
Identify monthly and yearly sales trends.
Create visualizations to communicate key findings.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

📐 Key Business Calculations
Total Sales = Quantity × Unit Price
Total Cost = Quantity × Unit Cost
Profit = Total Sales − Total Cost
Profit Margin = Profit ÷ Total Sales
